# Born for fish'n

A small fishing game for kids. Throw a line, wind it back past a fish, sell the catch, and buy gear.

Twelve spots from Home Pond to Champion Water. Catch every fish in a spot to open the next one. A cleared spot stays clear. Fish wander the pond and linger near snags, so casts can risk the line. The dotted circle shows cast reach; buy a fishing pole to throw further.

Open `index.html` on a phone or iPad. Money, gear, and your best catch are saved on the device. No account and no ads.
