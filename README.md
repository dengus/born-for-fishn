# Born for fish'n

A small fishing game for kids. Throw a line, wind it back past a fish, and get paid as soon as you catch it. Spend the money on gear.

Twelve spots from Home Pond to Champion Water. Catch every fish in a spot to open the next one. A cleared spot stays clear. Fish wander the pond and linger near snags, so casts can risk the line. The dotted circle shows cast reach; buy a fishing pole to throw further.

From Rocky Reach through Champion Water, cartoon crocodiles chase the fish. Storm Bend and Champion Water have more crocodiles, and they swim and eat faster. If a crocodile eats a fish before you catch every one, that spot starts again. Money, gear, and earlier spots stay.

Open `index.html` on a phone or iPad. Money, gear, and your best catch are saved on the device. No account and no ads.
