Born for fish'n

  1. Press Throw, or drag on the water, to cast. The dotted circle shows how far you can throw. Buy a fishing pole to cast further.
  2. Hold Wind, or drag back towards you, to bring the hook in.
  3. You catch a fish only when the hook passes close on the way in. Tapping a fish does not catch it.
  4. Keep fish in your basket, then sell them at the Shop.
  5. Catch every fish in this spot to open the next one.
